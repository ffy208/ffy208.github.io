Selected Research & Publications

On-Policy Distillation for Vision-Language Reasoning

Transferred on-policy distillation (DAgger-style, per-token KL to a teacher on the student's own rollouts) to Qwen3-VL on ChartQA. A 2B student trained on 300 questions matches supervised distillation on 3,000 (+5.8 points at equal data, paired 95% CI excludes 0), and a token-level analysis shows the teacher's feedback lands on answers and format before chart values.

FireFair: Equity-Adjusted Multi-Agent Triage for Wildfire Ignition Forecasting

FireFair audits a California wildfire forecaster by the CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index, cuts the equal-opportunity gap by 75% at a 1.6pp F1 cost, and routes marginal signals into visible multi-agent verification.

Lead developer of a road-condition data collection system for a state transportation agency, capturing time-synchronized video, 200 Hz IMU, and GPS streams through custom native modules on iOS and Android. Shipped multiple releases adding first-ever iOS support at full sensor parity with the legacy Android app, backed by a 274-test suite; rebuilt uploads as a streaming flow with stall detection and automatic retry, eliminating iOS out-of-memory crashes on multi-GB recordings.

AI onboarding assistant

Inductify

An AI-powered onboarding chatbot that lets new hires query 10K+ internal documents with cited answers under 5 seconds, backed by a RAG pipeline with ChromaDB retrieval and a CrossEncoder re-ranker. On a 54-question held-out set, top-3 answer accuracy rose from 2% (LLM only) to 83% (full pipeline).
